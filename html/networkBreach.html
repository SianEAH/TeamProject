Station 404 Breach

The brief: An employee left their workstation unlocked and their inbox is full of suspicious emails. You're the security officer covering the breach. Walk through three layers - the desk, the inbox, and the door keypad - and lock the station back down.

How it works: Every layer gives you a number. Add all three numbers together at the end to crack the door code. Watch the Clues Collected panel on the left, it fills in as you go.
