Fire Safety Training

Drag and Drop the correct type of fire extingusher for specific scenarios.

An essential component to fire safety is knowing what type of fire extinguisher to use in a given situation. If the incorrect fire extinguisher is used for the type of fire being dealt with, it runs the risk of only exasperating the situation.

In this puzzle, you will drag each kind of commonly used fire extinguisher to the correct situation.

Remember:

  • Water Extinguishers with red labels for carbon-based combustables, such as paper, wood or textiles.
  • Foam Extinguishers with cream labels for flammable liquids such as petrol, diesel or oil.
  • Dry Powder Extinguishers with blue labels in the prescence of flammable gasses, like propane or methane.
  • Carbon Dixoide, or CO2, Extinguishers with black labels for electrical fires.
  • Wet Chemical Extinguishers with yellow labels cooking oils and fats.
