Fire Safety Training

Puzzle 1: Pick the Correct Response

With regards to fire safety, the first actions taken can be extremely important. Making the right or wrong choices at the start can be the difference between people getting hurt and everyone making it out without a scratch.

In this puzzle, you will be presented with hyopthetical fire safety situations. Your task is to choose the best course of action from the multiple-choice options provided.

Instructions

  • Read the situation carefully.
  • Select the response that most adheres to fire safety standards.
  • Your score will be calculated as you progress through the module. You will not be told until the end.
  • Hints are available
  • You will be timed
  • You will receive a feedback form upon completion
