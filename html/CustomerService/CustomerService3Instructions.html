Click the sentences with the wrong responses

Getting a good first impression from a customer is very important in business. If the customer doesn't get a good impression, they may never use or visit your business again. This puzzle gives employees an insight into both good and bad pieces of dialogue within a customer interaction.

In this puzzle, you must read through the dialogue and spot the sentences that have responses that seem incorrect.
