Customer Service Training

Puzzle 1: Pick the Correct Greeting

In customer service, first impressions are extremely important. The way you greet a customer can determine whether their experience starts positively or negatively.

In this puzzle, you will be shown different customer situations. Your task is to choose the best greeting from the multiple-choice options provided.

Instructions

  • Read the customer situation carefully.
  • Select the greeting that provides the best customer service.
  • Your score will be calculated as you progress through the module. You will not be told until the end.
  • Hints are available
  • You will be timed
  • You will receive a feedback form upon completion
