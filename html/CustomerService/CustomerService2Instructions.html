Customer Service Training

Drag and Drop the Correct Order for Handling Complaints

Handling complaints professionally is an essential part of the customer service industry. It is an essential skill for employees. When customers raise issues, it is important to respond calmly and in a professional manner and follow the correct steps to handle the issue.

In this puzzle, you will drag each complaint to the most appropriate response. Think carefully about which response shows the best customer service.

Instructions

  • Read the customer complaint carefully.
  • Drag the complaint to the best response.
  • Your time and score will continue to be tracked.
  • Hints will also be available to you
